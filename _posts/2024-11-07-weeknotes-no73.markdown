As we are running down the weeks now till the end of the year, work is flowing heavily (outbound), coffee is getting back to a high level of taste (thanks to re-subscribing to Pact Coffee again!), and the weather sucks.

It's a weird vibe when you are working your notice when contracting; you are closing tickets, attending meetings and helping to shape future ways of working, knowing you won't be there to reap the rewards or see it through - but we all know the rules, leave the workplace better than you found it!

News of America's choice is again, quite jarring and something that I am scratching my head over - my bubble of friends in America seem to be the opposite of this choice, but.. we have to hope for the best.

<strong>Personal-wise:</strong><br>
I spent loads of "switch-off work" time with the kids; trick or treating was an absolute delight, cold.. but loved it. Learning to switch off pretty late in my career and reminding myself is a huge step for someone self-employed (as is my wife) is tough; it seems we are always thinking of the next contract or sale, which is anxiety-inducing, but I have to trust the process, the universe and.. <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/posts/michaelcattell_mikey-cattell-senior-digital-designer-interaction-activity-7259891726843064320-fIfj">my LinkedIn updates</a>!



<strong>Work-wise:</strong><br>
This isnt my first rodeo of handing over work, so that process had started well before it was meant to; onboarding the next (and excellent) designer has begun, knowing it's in safe and capable hands (and someone fresh on the project) is wonderful. I've got some minor tickets left, which feel minor, but I'm sure they will suddenly grow (as is always the case). 

<strong>What am I looking forward to this week:</strong><br>
All the work offers I shall receive, of course! 
On a serious note - I am looking for Service design and interaction design (let's throw in Product Design/UX in there, too) roles; I have done both many times, sometimes without being asked, but filling that gap that is often left out. 

Cooking bread, I'm desperate to cook bread!
