Work has been pretty hectic, but as normal, do not think that a moan - busy is good, moving in the direction of delivery and having a clear goal in sight is even better and we've got this. <br>

Will I need cups of coffee, new playlists on Spotify and regular snacks? YES. Send coffee, send playlists.

<strong>What have you all missed?</strong><br>
I handed over the latest design mocks, and had support and feedback throughout the way - but it's time to let it go and see what the wider team think before it gets close to development time, I fully expect and want feedback from all corners, rip it apart, kick it down the street - throw those rocks, often said to many folks - I'm the least precious designer ever, I want to deliver good work that is used by users and they know how to use it!

Working closely with the developers has been so good, let me rephrase that - these are some of the best developers I've worked with, working closely with great developers has been so good, they keep any design changes on the straight and narrow and allow me to plan better, design nearer to the actual build and this also allows me to keep any wild ideas in check (scope creep is a big no-no).
