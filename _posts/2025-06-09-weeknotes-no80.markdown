# **Morocco, Muses & Mural Transfers**

During my birthday week and holiday, I decided it was time to be much more visible on LinkedIn.

Over the past month (maybe even longer), I’m not sure why I haven’t posted much. Sometimes weeknotes slipped into *monthly* notes. I stopped sharing friends’ posts, commenting on them, or even being interested in what was going on in the industry.

Could I be tired of it all?  
Yes - you're damn right I am.  
But I feel experienced enough now to help steer an industry that feels quite lost. 

It’s less about shouting into the void, and more about *showing up with solutions*. Less moaning, more making. Less "what’s wrong with this?", and more “here’s what’s working”.

Shoutout to two good eggs:
- [Gavin Elliot](https://www.linkedin.com/in/gavinelliot/), who’s showing real optimism around the role of AI in design.
- [Tom Scott](https://www.linkedin.com/in/tomscottuk/), who’s still out here bringing knowledge to the masses.

So with that — you’ll see more of me. What I’m working on. What I’m thinking. Things I usually don't talk about for whatever reason.

Yes, it's one of *those* “I'm going to turn up more” posts...  
But honestly? It's overdue. And it's going to help me grow, express myself better, and sharpen my communication skills.
