## Re-brand, usual drama
<br>
[GOV.UK's new branding](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/changes-to-govuk) caught the eye of many people, loads concentrating on "the dot", which is a shame, usually as most re-brands go - it's natural for people to discuss the cost, the work etc, but there is more to come "under the hood" and I do think it's a pretty good re-brand. 

The point, among many, is to be more modern, attract a younger audience to apps, services and become (I would imagine) more self-serving, similar to a project [I worked on for NHS](/NHS-reverification.html) <em>(absolute shameless plug)</em> - people are, I believe, being led to owning their data more which I hope leads to better-designed services that are easier to use and less overwhelming.
