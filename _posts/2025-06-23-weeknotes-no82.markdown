## Good Read – Design in 2025
<br>
I’m a big fan of Tom and his writing. His recent post on [product design hiring in 2025](https://verifiedinsider.substack.com/p/product-design-hiring-in-2025) gave me a bit of a case-study-kick-up-the-arse. You know the drill, I am 2-3 years behind and needed it.

It’s also no surprise that the most desirable designers are all-rounders—I’ve seen that when hiring and being hired. The rise of the super IC feels very real.

There are loads of Lead-level designers knocking about at the moment, it makes me wonder if expectations around what a Lead *is* have shifted. (Tom’s first point made me pause and think this)

Anyway, it's worth a read, especially if you’re a designer thinking about your next move. Let me know what you think (and Tom, too). It’s an exciting (and slightly scary) time to be in the field.

## Work & Crit Wins
<br>
This week was filled with many discussions with developers, mostly around whether we could simplify or reduce design work to meet delivery deadlines.

I like those challenges. I genuinely believe in "less is best", especially when we don’t have strong evidence a thing was even wanted. When you weigh effort vs value, a lot of work just isn’t worth dying on any hill.

I’d rather ship something meaningful than waste time polishing a feature that maybe isnt needed as much as we think. If people *do* care, they’ll let us know — and we’ll iterate.

But it wasn’t all “What can we cut?” chat. We had a cracking design crit session where minds stretched, devs felt heard, we found a smart way through a tricky problem, and even squeezed in a “let’s do more of this” moment.
