<strong>Work-check:</strong> With the news of my contract ending in December, it's been a crunch of delivering design tickets, which are tickets from my backlog that I had parked for those quiet months. I think when I get a chance and look at what has been delivered over my time here (esp the last 3 months) and the workaround educating the value of design - it'll be a feeling of satisfaction and err warmth of a job-done-well. 

One thing I have felt in this contract compared to my others, is the lack of backup of a pre-set design system or design-ways-of-working in regards to work being set, handovers and the rules around that (when done is done, why and when we need to iterate), there have been many obstacles and issues around speed, expectations and what-is-done from the design team, and including sign-off. We also use Jira a lot more than in any team I've been part of, it's become a workload of truth - but design-isnt-like-that (which is another post). It's felt more like a chase and an exhausting one, which I haven't enjoyed but it's the nature of the beast and something i've been chipping away at with a design-system of sorts, but the decision to start with desktop only designs has bit us in the bum a number of times, nevermind the dark-mode requirements too. It hasn't been all doom and gloom - far from it in fact, we have changed a lot in the time we've worked together, we've got consistency in design, we've started to iterate in parts (no big sweep changes) and also we have started to shape value in our work.
