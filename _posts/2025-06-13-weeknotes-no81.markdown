## 🏢 Remote Working vs. Office (Again)
<br>
Another was the discussion around remote working against coming into the office - this will never leave us - and one I touched on during a recent podcast I appeared in somewhere (wait and see!). 

I won't discuss my full opinion, but needless to say, I believe it's incredibly short-sighted to think a request to go back into an office will suddenly improve output at the cost of many things. It lowers access for people (parents, those with accessibility requirements etc). In the last 3 years, I've never worked with "my team" in the same county - nevermind in the same office!

## 🧪 National Rollout Prototype Progress
<br>
I’m still working on the final prototype for a national rollout - a project that will support millions of claimants and hundreds of work coaches.

It’s not easy. I’m in more than one team, and a lot has changed since I last picked it up - from little tweaks to full flow changes. But this isn’t just a “testing prototype” anymore. It’s become:

- A training platform for Ops  
- A source of truth for documentation  
- A way to promote the service, inform future investment, and show “how to do it”

I always aim to leave the team with a clear, archived “here’s what we delivered” flow - so future teams can understand why and how we did it, without relying on a fragile live prototype (which is currently a nightmare to update due to security blocks!).
