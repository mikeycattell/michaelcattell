## Contracting life
<br>
To kick off the week, early on, there was a discussion around the future of contracting within my department, which was handled brilliantly by the Lead and reminded me of the ups and downs of leadership (something I miss more and more as my time in contracting goes on).

This will eventually lead to the "green sash" on LinkedIn, but I am unsure about the short term—I am contracted until January 2026. I also realise this is the lifestyle we choose as contracting and completely understand, something I often tell those who seem to lust after contracting—you must be comfortable putting yourself out there and also be vulnerable to plans changing upon a throw of a dice.
